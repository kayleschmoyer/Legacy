Breakpoints

Seven named ranges, mirrored from the Tamagui media defaults consumed by @tamagui/config. Use them as $gtXs, $gtSm, $gtMd, $gtLg, $gtXl on web (the "greater-than" form is the convention in web/src) and via useMedia() in shared components. Dark theme is primary at every size.

Conventions for our core surfaces

scoreboard / matchup grid — three columns at $gtLg, two at $gtSm, single stack below. Mirrors MatchupGrid.tsx ($gtSm: 50%, $gtLg: 33.333%).

stats tables — true <table> at $gtSm and above; transform to per-player cards below, with the headline stat (AVG / ERA) pulled into a .score position.

draft board — full grid only at $gtLg. Below that, collapse to a single round-by-round timeline with the on-the-clock card pinned to the top.

primary nav — top bar shows full nav at $gtMd; below that, a hamburger drawer (see app.tsx). Hit targets stay at 44px on touch.

brackets / fixtures — horizontally scroll within a fixed-height container at ≤ md; never reflow round columns into rows (it breaks the metaphor).

video player — locks 16:9, full-bleed at ≤ sm, capped at 880px at $gtMd.
