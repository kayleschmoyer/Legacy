01 / Common Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Eight of the most-pulled threads from our Discord, answered.

From My Leagues, tap + New League. You'll pick an era — Dead-Ball (1900-1919), Live-Ball (1920-1941), Integration (1947-1968), Free Agency (1976-1993), Steroid (1994-2004), or Modern (2005-Present) — set roster size and scoring, and invite up to 11 other managers by email or share-link.

Drafts can be live (snake or auction) or asynchronous over 7 days. Most first-time commissioners pick a 12-team Modern era snake draft to start.

Game-by-game and play-by-play archives sourced from the official MLB statistics feed and Retrosheet. Modern-season games refresh throughout the day; historical seasons (1901-2024) are static and verified.

Every player in your league's era window is in the pool. Pick Babe Ruth (1927) and Mike Trout (2019) on the same roster — their performance is scored against the actual season they played, not normalized.

Commissioners can amend rules with a majority vote of active managers. Changes take effect the following Monday at 12:00 PM ET. Categories (BA, HR, RBI, SB, OBP, SLG, etc.) are locked from week 14 onward.

Auto-draft kicks in based on your pre-ranked queue. If you didn't rank, the system picks from a default era-balanced list. You can edit the queue right up until your first pick is on the clock.

Trades require both managers to accept and pass a 24-hour league veto window. Waivers run on a FAAB (free-agent acquisition budget) of $100 per season, processed Wednesday at 3:00 AM ET.

Yes — iOS and Android apps share your account, your roster, and your draft queue. Same login (Google or Discord). The web app and the mobile app stay in sync second-by-second during live drafts.

Profile Settings → Account → Delete. Active leagues will keep your team as Manager Departed until the season ends, then archive everything. Account deletion is permanent after a 14-day cooling-off period.

02 / The Archive

Historical stats — how it works.

A few of the deeper questions about era play, era-adjusted scoring, and the records we count.

By default, no. A 1968 Bob Gibson start scores against his actual line — 1.12 ERA and all. League settings let commissioners enable era-normalized scoring that adjusts to the league average of each season; this evens the playing field but feels less honest to the history. Most leagues run un-adjusted.

Negro Leagues statistics (1920-1948) were officially recognized as Major League records in 2020. We include all qualified players — Josh Gibson, Satchel Paige, Cool Papa Bell — with the same eligibility as their AL/NL contemporaries. Stats are sourced from the Seamheads Negro Leagues Database.

Your league's scoring rules apply on top of the raw line. A 3-for-4 with a HR scores differently in points leagues vs. categories leagues vs. roto. Tap any score to see the breakdown — the math is always shown.

2005-Present. Live games update within 90 seconds of the official MLB feed. Off-day stats and lineup changes refresh hourly.

Optional. Default leagues run regular-season-only. Commissioners can enable a October bonus week that scores LDS, LCS, and World Series games at 1.5× weight.
